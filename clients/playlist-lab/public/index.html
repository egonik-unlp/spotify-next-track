Session generators

Autoregressive journeys from one seed, under one shared retrieval policy.

Seed

Spotify URIs, bare track ids, open.spotify.com URLs or vocab indices — one per line. More seed tracks give the model more context.

Models

Both slots run the same seed and the same policy, so the only difference is the algorithm.

Retrieval policy

Units are pool-local score z, so one value means the same displacement for every model. Temperature 0 is greedy.

One journey costs about one artifact load (~8 s including the calibration pass over your own sessions), whatever its length.

How to read a journey

Mood card
The primary readout: where the journey sits on axes you can hear — energy, valence, tempo, era, and your own history with these exact tracks. Each row's rail shades the 10th–90th percentile of the same statistic measured on your real listening sessions, marks your median, and ticks your seed. A value inside the band means the generator moves the way you actually listen; outside is not automatically wrong, but it is the thing to go listen for. Acoustics cover ~74% of the corpus, so every row prints the count it used.
Continuity
The group that answers does this algorithm conserve a vibe. It is about transitions, not averages: a journey can average your seed's energy exactly while alternating 0.2 and 0.9 track by track, which is the lurch you hear and the mean hides.
Journey shape
The same movement as a picture: one chart, both journeys overlaid on shared axes (energy, valence, tempo, era) against your seed's dashed baseline. Flat holds the mood, sawtooth lurches. Hover any point for the track it belongs to.
Over
The runner-up tracks each step turned down, by name, with how many candidates were genuinely in contention. no real contest means the step had one obvious answer; a dozen in contention means the pick was near-arbitrary and a re-roll would sound different. Hover a name for its genre, year and how far it scored from the pick.
Leaderboard facets
Folded away under each panel: H, mood_coh, ild and the repetition flags, computed on the journey you are hearing so they reconcile with what the leaderboard ranks on. Cosines in a 517-d fused space — comparable across models, unconfirmable by ear, which is why they are no longer the headline.
